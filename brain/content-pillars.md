# Content Pillars — Frankly Human

Recurring, reusable pillar *categories* per track — generalized from the "Content Pillar" column in `business-planning/Content_Ideas_Spreadsheet.csv`. This file is evergreen; it defines the themes to rotate through. It does **not** duplicate the CSV's specific post ideas, hooks, platform variants, or draft status — that CSV is the living operational backlog Component 2's pipeline should pull concrete briefs from. When a brief names a track and asks for "a post," check the CSV first for an existing idea in that track before inventing a new angle; if generating a genuinely new angle, file it back into a pillar below.

---

## Come Home to Yourself

Pillars trace directly to D2M's 12 modules (see `curriculum-d2m.md` for full module content):

- Origin Story (Module 1) — the unconscious "life brief" you inherited
- Values in Practice (Module 2)
- Identity & Self-Concept (Module 3)
- Energy & Capacity (Module 4) — energy leaks vs. real constraints
- Life Systems (Module 5)
- Future Self (Module 6)
- Decision Design (Module 7)
- Prototyping Your Life (Module 8)
- Boundaries & Emotional Labour (Module 9) — scripts you can actually say
- Relationships & Belonging (Module 10)
- Ambition & Desire (Module 11) — desires quietly edited out
- Next Chapter & Life OS (Module 12)
- Objection-handling: "I like my life and don't want to blow it up" — reassurance content, not a hard sell

## Regional Business Leader

Pillars trace to Business Design Reset's parallel 12 modules (see `curriculum-business-design-reset.md`) plus regional-specific framing:

## Design-Led: Practitioner

Pillars trace to the Design Thinking Diagnostic and the 13-module curriculum (see `curriculum-design-led-practitioner.md`):

- The three lenses — Desirable / Viable / Feasible, and which one gets skipped under pressure
- The six design mindsets — which is hardest to practise, and why
- Diagnostic teaser / "building in public" content ahead of the tool's launch
- Skill tagged by dread, not love — the capability-vs-enjoyment split
- Module-specific pillars as the curriculum surfaces them: Research as habit, insight vs. observation, empowered vs. feature teams, DevEx as design, service blueprinting, agent-era IA, conversational UI, AI-era trust signals, dashboard/data clarity, judgment traps

## Design-Led: Leader

Pillars trace to the Design Leadership Diagnostic and 13-module curriculum (see `curriculum-design-led-leader.md`):

- The Silent Expert — the counter-argument you had and didn't say
- "Great work isn't sufficient" — credibility without a seat at the table
- The four threads of influence: Budget Authority, Roadmap Influence, Executive Trust, Relationship Capital
- The three traps: Service Department Trap, Feature Factory Manager, Silent Expert
- Capability vs. credibility thesis (from `Design_Led_POV_Manifesto.md`) — flagship, evergreen post material
- Relationship Capital specifically — "the fourth thing nobody likes to say out loud"

## How to use this file with a content brief

A Component 2 brief should specify **topic + track + platform**. Use this file to pick or validate the pillar the topic falls under, `positioning.md` for register, `brand-voice.md` for sentence-level voice rules, and the matching `curriculum-*.md` for factual grounding (so Claude draws on real module content instead of inventing it). Check `Content_Ideas_Spreadsheet.csv` for whether a similar idea already exists and its draft status before generating from scratch.
