# Curriculum Context — Design to Me (D2M)

Track: **Come Home to Yourself**. Source: `curriculum/workbooks/d2m/index.html`, `curriculum/d2m/Come_Home_to_Yourself_Sales_Page_DRAFT.md`. All content sourced directly from these files — nothing in this file is invented.

**12 modules, 3 layers, ~27.5 hours total.** This is the *current* structure — D2M was restructured from an older 13-module / 4-layer version (old Module 12 "Designing Your Next Chapter" and Module 13 "Integration/Life OS" merged into one module; the "Integration" layer folded into "Agency"). Business Design Reset (`curriculum-business-design-reset.md`) mirrors this new 12/3 structure, not the old one.

**The core claim:** Your life is a system. Systems produce predictable outcomes. If you want different outcomes, you don't need more willpower — you need a different system.

**Entry point:** The Why Workbook — a short guided reflection producing a Why Statement ("a draft, not a destination — a compass, not a contract").

## Who it's for

Mid-career professionals — competent by every measure, quietly unsure if this is the life they meant to build. Not broken, just ready for a different system. People who've done the personal-development reading and still can't act on it. People tired of optimising, ready to redesign.

## What participants leave able to do

- **See clearly** — patterns, values, constraints, emotional labour, systems.
- **Decide wisely** — decision hygiene, principled choices, energy-honouring, boundaries, next right move.
- **Act intentionally** — prototype changes, build rituals, sustainable rhythms, a Life OS.

## Voice notes specific to this curriculum

- Not a self-help course, not a productivity course, not a "fix yourself" course — "a thinking course for your life."
- "Design to Me is the personal counterpart to Design-Led. Where Design-Led teaches you how to make good decisions in complex organisations, D2M teaches you how to make good decisions in the complex organisation of your life."
- No AI/judgment framing — that positioning is reserved exclusively for the Design-Led tracks. D2M content should never mention AI or "judgment-building" language.
- Objection handling already drafted (from the sales page Q+A): "This isn't a demolition. It's less, but better." — for people worried the program means blowing up a life they're mostly happy with.
- Q+A also covers: not knowing what you want (normal, not exceptional), time constraints (a weekly hour, not a life overhaul), being close to burnout (rest first — the program assumes baseline capacity, not a substitute for recovery), and the honest no-guarantee framing (guarantees process, not outcome).
