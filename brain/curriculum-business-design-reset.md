Track: **Regional Business Leader**. Source: `business-planning/Business_Design_Reset_Curriculum.docx` (2026 Edition — extracted this session; the file was binary and unread until now, so treat this as freshly sourced, not previously verified against other docs). All content below sourced directly — nothing invented.

**12 modules, 3 layers — adapted directly from the current D2M structure** (Awareness → Alignment → Agency). This corrects an earlier assumption (in an older sales-page draft note) that Business Design Reset still used D2M's *pre-restructure* 13-module / 4-layer shape — the actual 2026 Edition doc confirms it has already been updated to mirror new D2M's 12/3 structure.

**The core claim:** Your business is a system — and systems produce predictable outcomes. If you want different outcomes, you don't need more hustle. You need a different system.

**Entry point:** Business Why Workshop — a 45–60 minute opener (shorter than D2M's Why Workbook; this audience has less appetite for deep interior work up front), ending with a bridge question into business strategy rather than staying purely reflective.

| D2M Why Workshop phase | Business Why Workshop version |
|---|---|
| Reframe | What does "success" mean for a business built on your terms — not the metro playbook? |
| Values | Non-Negotiables — what won't you compromise on to grow? |
| Moments of Truth | Turning Points — the decisions that shaped the business you're actually running |
| Why Statement | Business Why Statement — the purpose driving the business, not just "make more money" |

## Who it's for

Regional business owners building on their own terms, without performing someone else's version of success. Regional-specific angles the curriculum names explicitly: isolation, wearing every hat with no backup, no one to hand things to, being pressure-tested by late payers and busy seasons, living in the same town as your customers.

## Voice notes specific to this curriculum

- "Grounded but not soft. Practical but not clinical. Honest about regional realities, not generic. Structured but not rigid. Human, direct, and useful."
- Same "system, not hustle" core claim as D2M, applied to the business rather than the self.
