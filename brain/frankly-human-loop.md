# The Frankly Human Loop & the Coaching Flow

Two related but distinct frameworks are in play across Frankly Human's tools. Don't conflate them — they serve different purposes and different content types.

---

## 1. The Frankly Human Loop — curriculum structure

**See → Sense → Shape → Show → Sustain**

Source: `curriculum/workbooks/d2m/index.html`. This is the methodology D2M's 12 modules are built on — "every exercise moves you through one or more phases of this loop. The loop isn't linear — you'll revisit phases as you go deeper."

| Phase | Job | D2M tools associated |
|---|---|---|
| **See** | Discover what's really going on. Surface patterns, stories, and truths you've been living around. | Origin story · Values · Identity · Energy · Patterns · Life systems |
| **Sense** | Make meaning from what you see. Identify patterns, reframe problems, clarify what matters and why. | Decision hygiene · Problem framing · Belief flips · Why statement · Impact mapping |
| **Shape** | Design possibilities. Generate options, explore future selves, define ambitions, set constraints, choose the next right move. | Future self mapping · Opportunity canvas · Ambition design · Boundary scripts · 3 future stories |
| **Show** | Prototype, test, and learn. Run small experiments, gather real data, iterate your story. | 7-day prototype · Feedforward · Test & learn loops · Resilience building |
| **Sustain** | Build your Life Operating System. Rituals, rhythms, boundaries, metrics, and non-negotiables that hold. | Life OS · Personal manifesto · 90-day plan · MLF Canvas · Habit loops |

**Use this for:** content that draws on or references the D2M/Business Design Reset methodology directly — e.g. a post structured around one phase, or content tagging that maps a post to "See" vs "Sustain" content.

**Approved translation language for design-jargon-adjacent Loop terms (STYLE_GUIDE §10.1):** Prototype · Experiment · Next right move · Constraints · Systems · Friction · Patterns · Leverage points · Future self · Design your life · Make life flow — these land as sticky, repeatable vocabulary. Full translation table lives in `style-guides/STYLE_GUIDE.md` §10.1.

**Design-Led's parallel version:** The Design-Led Practitioner curriculum's Capstone module (13) introduces "**The Design-Led Loop**" as its own repeatable pattern (Frame → Learn → Make sense → Decide → Design → Test → Integrate → Repeat, per `curriculum/design-led-practitioner/Design_Led_Practitioner_Full_Curriculum.pdf`). This is a distinct, longer loop built for organisational problem-solving, not the same as the Frankly Human Loop above — don't merge the two when generating Design-Led content.

## 2. Discovery → Meaning → Action → Integration — the coaching conversation flow

Source: `business-planning/Diagnostic_Tool_Build_Notes.md`, `business-planning/_dtd_curriculum_extract.txt`, `leader_extract.txt`. This is the four-stage architecture used by every **conversational AI coaching tool** Frankly Human runs — the Why Workbook, the Business Why Workshop, the Design Thinking Diagnostic, and the Design Leadership Diagnostic all share this shape, with different content at each stage per track.

| Stage | Goal |
|---|---|
| **Discovery** | Surface the raw material (a project, a moment, a scored lens, a value) |
| **Meaning** | Understand why the gap or pattern exists |
| **Action** | Name one moment where the gap actually cost something concrete |
| **Integration** | Name the pattern and the clear line to what comes next |

**This is also the shape of Kit's six-email nurture sequences** (`Kit_Email_Content_Strategy.md`) — stretched to nurture pace rather than single-session pace. Email 1 delivers the result (Discovery/Meaning already happened in the tool), Email 2–3 go deeper (Meaning → Action), Email 4 is belief/POV (Integration groundwork), Email 5–6 are the invite (Integration → next step).

**Use this for:** any conversational tool spec, or any nurture-sequence content — briefs for Component 2 that specify "email 3 of the Come Home to Yourself sequence" should draw on this shape, not the five-phase Loop.

---

## Which one to use when generating content

- Referencing a specific D2M/Business Design Reset module or the "life is a system" methodology → **Frankly Human Loop**.
- Referencing the diagnostic tools, the Why Workbook, or nurture email sequences → **Discovery/Meaning/Action/Integration**.
- Design-Led Practitioner/Leader curriculum content, module-level → **Frankly Human Loop phase tags** (each Design-Led module lists a Practitioner-equivalent and, in Leader's case, which of the four threads/three traps it strengthens — see `curriculum-design-led-leader.md`), but the **program-level integration concept** is the separate Design-Led Loop, not the Frankly Human Loop.
