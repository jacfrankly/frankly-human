# Positioning — Frankly Human

Four audience tracks, replacing the AI-brain build brief's original two-track model (which predates the current business structure). Sourced from `style-guides/STYLE_GUIDE.md` §02–03/§10, `business-planning/Kit_Email_Content_Strategy.md`, `business-planning/Kit_Airtable_Wiring_Spec.md`, `business-planning/Frankly_Human_Promotions_Reference.md`, and the curriculum source docs. Content generation for any track should load this file plus the matching `curriculum-*.md` file.

**Do not use "AI-Era Design Leader"** as a track name — it isn't current terminology anywhere in the business docs. The correct names are below.

---

## Come Home to Yourself

**Entry tool → flagship program:** Why Workbook → Design to Me (D2M), 12 modules / 3 layers.

**Who it's for:** Mid-career professionals — often women, often in high-achieving environments — competent by every measure, quietly unsure if this is the life they meant to build. Per `curriculum/workbooks/d2m/index.html`: "not broken, just ready for a different system."

**They need to feel:** Understood, not fixed. Given tools, not prescriptions.

**Register (STYLE_GUIDE §03, §10):** Highest warmth of any track. Gentler sentence edge than Design-Led. Provocation style is personal pattern / unnamed recognition, not business scenario + data. Never diagnoses, prescribes, or fixes. Design language lands as metaphor, never jargon (see STYLE_GUIDE §10.1's translation table — "ideation" → "generate possibilities," etc.).

**Bio (community-facing, per `Frankly_Human_Promotions_Reference.md`):** all three certifications (Certified Life Coach, Human Design Practitioner, Mindfulness Practitioner) fit naturally here — reflective, values-based context, not a commercial-trust-building one.

**Channel:** Instagram / community / email over LinkedIn (per `Content_Ideas_Spreadsheet.csv` — D2M content is "optional, softened heavily" for LinkedIn).

## Regional Business Leader

**Entry tool → flagship program:** Business Why Workshop → Business Design Reset, 12 modules / 3 layers (adapted directly from D2M's structure — see `curriculum-business-design-reset.md`). Feeds the Regional Business Leadership Day (rotating Gippsland towns: Maffra, Warragul, Bairnsdale, Sale).

**Who it's for:** Regional business owners building on their own terms — not the metro playbook. Explicitly not "Founder."

**Register — thinner source material than the other three tracks [flagged as inferred]:** Per `Content_Ideas_Spreadsheet.csv`: "more direct than D2M, less sharp than Design-Led" — regional-business-owner register. Per the curriculum doc itself: "Grounded but not soft. Practical but not clinical. Honest about regional realities, not generic." STYLE_GUIDE.md doesn't document this track at all yet (it only covers DESIGN-LED, D2M, and Service Blueprinting) — this register is inferred from the Kit email sequence and curriculum doc, not yet pressure-tested at scale. Worth confirming against real reactions once the Maffra day runs, per the same "worth testing this concern against real reactions" instinct `Frankly_Human_Promotions_Reference.md` applies elsewhere.

**Bio:** Regional Business Leader bios should lead with design-leadership and enterprise career credibility, per `Frankly_Human_Promotions_Reference.md` — same caution as Design-Led on Human Design specifically (risks undercutting the "not a soft issue" positioning this track shares with Design-Led).

**Channel:** Regional Facebook groups are likely the primary channel (per `Content_Ideas_Spreadsheet.csv`), plus local event pages for the Leadership Day.

## Design-Led: Practitioner

**Entry tool → flagship program:** Design Thinking Diagnostic → Design-Led: Practitioner, 13 modules (Foundations, Research, Analysis, Strategy, Product, DevEx & Product Intelligence, Service Design, Information Architecture, Conversational UI, AI-Era Design, Data & Decisions, Judgment in Practice, Capstone/Integration).

**Who it's for:** Mid-weight designers who keep getting treated as an executor, not a strategic partner. Entry pain is professional ("I keep getting treated as an executor"), not personal.

**Register (STYLE_GUIDE §03, DESIGN-LED baseline):** Direct, commercially fluent, "a light wink." Per `_dtd_curriculum_extract.txt`: "warm clarity with a backbone... commercially fluent, with a light wink. This audience responds to precision and a little edge, not encouragement for its own sake."

**They need to feel:** Seen, challenged, equipped — not lectured at, not given theory they can't use Monday.

---

## Design-Led: Leader

**Entry tool → flagship program:** Design Leadership Diagnostic → Design-Led: Leader, 13 modules, built on four threads of influence (Budget Authority, Roadmap Influence, Executive Trust, Relationship Capital) and three traps (Service Department Trap, Feature Factory Manager, Silent Expert).

**Who it's for:** Heads of Design and senior design leaders who've built the credibility and still can't get the room. Entry pain is a level up from Practitioner: not "am I good at my job" but "why does the business still treat design as something to be managed, not something that shapes strategy."

**Register — pitched higher than Practitioner [inferred, see `brand-voice.md`]:** Per `leader_extract.txt`: "Skip the warm-up — this audience wants precision fast, not permission to begin." Less "let's explore this together," more "here's what I see, tell me if I'm wrong." No self-paced version — this audience needs structured cohort delivery.

**Delivery ladder:** Diagnostic ($500) → Group Program ($5,000) → Executive Coaching ($15,000) → Corporate Cohort Licensing ($25,000–$50,000).

## Cross-track rules

- **"Founder" is rejected everywhere** — too tech-coded. This applies to all four tracks, not just Design-Led.
- **Track-specific bios, not one universal bio** (`Frankly_Human_Promotions_Reference.md`): community-facing tracks (Come Home to Yourself) can carry all three certifications; enterprise-facing tracks (Regional Business Leader, both Design-Led) should lead with design-leadership and career credibility, with real caution around including Human Design specifically.
- **Kit tagging** (per `Kit_Email_Content_Strategy.md`): every subscriber gets a `track:*` tag (one of the four above, using these exact slugs: `come-home-to-yourself`, `regional-business-leader`, `design-led-practitioner`, `design-led-leader`) plus a `stage:*` tag. Content generation briefs should specify track using these same names so output maps cleanly to Kit segments.
