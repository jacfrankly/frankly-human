- Business Analyst — business case, ROI model (~£90k–£160k/year savings, ~25-month payback), value proposition

**Wave 2 (parallel):**
- Strategy Architect — service vision, North Star metric, principles, strategic tensions
- Risk Assessor — risk register (25 risks across 5 categories), 5 critical assumptions, go/no-go recommendations

**Wave 3 (parallel):**
- Requirements Writer — service blueprint (8 stages × 6 process layers), 7 feature user stories, full PRD, 5 touchpoint briefs, edge case inventory
- Synthesis agent — 3 × AS-IS/TO-BE journey maps, full recommendations report

## Critical Programme Pre-Conditions

These must be resolved before the pilot launches:

1. **Service owner appointed** — named individual, Director-level authority over all six teams
2. **Volume validated** — pull 24 months of Jira data across all six teams; validate 150–300 req/year assumption
3. **Channel decommission commitments** — written sign-off from all six team leads with line manager countersignature

**Fast-track pathway is a no-go until additionally:**
4. Internal risk review approval received
5. Cybersecurity and Legal define fast-track criteria (not ratify them)
6. Triage logic uses verifiable objective criteria only — no requester self-declaration
7. Post-onboarding audit mechanism designed before pathway launches

## North Star Metric

**Requester-confirmed resolution rate within target timeframe (without chasing or escalating)**

Measured as: % of closed requests where (a) elapsed time within target band + (b) no requester escalation/chase + (c) requester confirms outcome was actionable.

Current baseline: **unknown** — extracting this from Jira is a programme pre-condition.
