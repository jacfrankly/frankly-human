2 — Critical assumptions & open questions status

Five critical assumptions from the Risk Assessor and nine open questions from the Design Spec. Fill in the Status column after each stakeholder conversation. Mark each as CONFIRMED / CONTRADICTED / UPDATED / STILL OPEN and note the source.

ID Assumption / Question Stakeholder status (fill in)
CA-01 A Director-level service owner can be named before programme start with formal authority over all six teams.
CA-02 All six teams will decommission their existing Jira intake channels when the front door launches.
CA-03 Fast-track triage criteria can be defined objectively using verifiable fields — not requester self-declaration.
CA-04 Request volume is 150–300 per year across all six teams (unvalidated — Jira export not yet done).
CA-05 Six existing Jira instances can be integrated to a common routing layer (JSM platform confirmed).
OQ-01 Who is the confirmed service owner with Director-level authority over all six teams?
OQ-02 What is the agreed SLA for each team's review per request type (standard and fast-track)?
OQ-03 Decision authority model: does one team's objection block the whole request, or does the service owner adjudicate?
OQ-04 What is the appeals process for a requester who disputes a rejection?
OQ-05 What notification channels are required beyond email and in-app (e.g. Teams integration, SMS)?
OQ-06 What is the data retention policy for request records including rejected and withdrawn requests?
OQ-07 Will the pilot include all six teams or a subset? If a subset, which teams and app types are in scope?
OQ-08 Process and authority for retiring or updating an existing catalogue record (e.g. app later found to pose new risk)?
OQ-09 Are there app types explicitly out of scope (e.g. internally developed apps, infrastructure tools)?
