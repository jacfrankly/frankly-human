Section 01

5 mistakes people inadvertently make when trying to live a purposeful life.

None of these are character flaws. They're patterns — and patterns can be redesigned.

01
5 Mistakes · The patterns

These show up quietly — and they compound.

Recognising the pattern is the first move. Not judging it — seeing it.

… is that me?

  • Waiting until things are "perfect" to start

    Purpose doesn't arrive finished. It's built through imperfect action.

  • Letting other people's values steer the ship

    Living by borrowed values feels fine — until it doesn't.

  • Confusing ambition with purpose

    Chasing achievement and chasing meaning aren't the same chase.

  • Skipping the emotional data

    Your feelings are information. Ignoring them costs more than listening to them.

  • Believing the "right time" exists

    It doesn't. There's only now — and what you choose to do with it.

Section 04

What's
my purpose?

You've done the work. Now we put it into one statement.

04
Crafting My Why · The framework

Two parts. That's the whole framework.

To…
[Take these actions]
What you do — the specific, repeatable thing.
So that…
[I have this impact]
Why it matters — to others, not just to you.

Example

To authentically share my gifts — so that others may realise their gifts can make a difference.

This is an ongoing process. Tweak it as you prototype your life — as priorities shift, as you grow.

#imperfect-action
Activity · Write it
Activity 12 minutes

Write your why statement. First draft only.

  • Start with "To…" — anchor the action in your top 3 values.
  • Complete "So that…" — name the impact on someone other than yourself.
  • Read it aloud. If it sounds like a LinkedIn bio, rewrite it.
  • Don't wait for the perfect version. Something true beats something polished.

I hear you.

Three things I hear every time.

Objection 01
"I love my work. I don't need to blow it all up."

Design to Me is not about uprooting your career. It's not about adding more for the sake of more. It's about finding what's missing — and building that in, without the drama.

Objection 02
"I have no idea what I want."

That's exactly the right starting point. Not knowing is data too. The work is to get curious about what you'd want if you let yourself have an opinion about your own life.

Objection 03
"I literally don't have the time."

You can't make more time. But you can make choices. You can ask for help. You can let the washing be folded the wrong way once a week. The washing gets done. It's imperfect. That's fine.
