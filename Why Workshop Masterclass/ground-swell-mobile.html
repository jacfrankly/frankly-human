04
Crafting Your Boundaries · The framework

Two parts. That's the whole framework.

I'm no longer available for…
[What you're done carrying]
The specific, honest thing.
What I need instead…
[What you want more of]
Name it — not just the lack.
Example

'I'm not available for last-minute requests that undermine my rest. I need 48 hours' notice.'

Say it once. Kindly. Clearly. You don't need to justify it.

Activity · Write it
Activity 12 minutes

Write your boundary. First draft only.

  • Start with 'I'm no longer available for…'
  • Complete 'What I need instead is…'
  • Read it aloud. If it sounds like an apology, rewrite it.
  • Say it as if you believe it.

I hear you.
I hear you.

Three things I hear every time.

→ Objection 01
"I love my life. I don't need to change everything."

Ground Swell is not about uprooting your life. It's about finding what's missing — and gently building that in, without the drama.

→ Objection 02
"I have no idea what I want."

That's exactly the right starting point. Not knowing is data. The work is getting curious about what you'd want if you let yourself have an opinion about your own life.

→ Objection 03
"I literally don't have the time."

You can't make more time. But you can make choices. You can let the washing be folded the wrong way once a week. It still gets done — imperfectly. That's fine.

The question
"

What possibilities could use a bit of gentle design right now — in small and easy ways?

Not everything. Not the whole life. Just one place — where a softer, clearer eye might make the next step easier.
