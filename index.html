How I work — the Frankly Human Loop
See

Discover what's really going on — surface the patterns, stories, and truths you've been living around.

Sense

Make meaning from what you see. Reframe the problem. Get clear on what matters and why.

Shape

Design possibilities. Generate options, explore future selves, choose the next right move.

Show

Prototype, test, and learn. Run small experiments, gather real data, iterate the story.

Sustain

Build the systems that hold — rituals, rhythms, boundaries, and non-negotiables.
