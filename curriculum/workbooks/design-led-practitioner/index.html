What Design‑Led is

Design-Led is a program about judgment — the kind that shapes products, services, teams, and organisations. It's not a design course. It's a thinking course.

It's about how to see clearly, decide wisely, and act intentionally in complex systems full of humans, constraints, politics, and possibility.

"AI compressed the execution layer of design. It didn't compress judgment — and judgment is where the seat at the table lives now."

Who it's for

  • Mid-weight designers tired of being treated as executors, not strategic partners
  • Designers who want to build real product-management capability, not just the language of it
  • Designers who keep getting the credit for craft and skipped for strategy
  • Teams who want a shared discipline for research, analysis, and decision-making
  • Anyone who wants to be impossible for a product manager to out-judge
The entry point

Start with the diagnostic

Before the full curriculum, the Design Thinking Diagnostic names the specific pattern keeping you in an executor role — a scored read on your last real project across the three lenses, your weakest design mindset, and a direct line to the module that addresses it.

A 20–30 minute AI-coached conversation. Produces a Design Influence Profile — a diagnosis, not a fix.

How it works

Three ways to use this

01

Present it

Each module is a scroll-snap presentation deck. Open in Present mode to facilitate a half-day session. Facilitator notes are pinned at the bottom of each slide.

02

Work through it

Switch to Workbook mode and the page transforms — teaching content collapses, activities open, your responses save automatically. Print a clean A4 workbook to take home.

03

Go deeper with Claude

Each module has a draft Claude coaching spec — exercises, deepening prompts, and distress protocols. Not wired to a live tool yet, but ready for when it is.
