What Design‑Led: Leader is

Design-Led: Leader is a program about organisational influence — the kind that determines whether a design function stays a service department or becomes the function that shapes how the business thinks.

It's not a leadership style course. It's not a communications course. It's not Practitioner with more seniority in the room. It's a program about power, structure, and the specific, nameable patterns that keep senior design leaders credible but sidelined.

"The seat at the table now goes to whoever can hold judgment across the whole decision — not defend one discipline's slice of it."

Who it's for

  • Heads of Design and senior design leaders tired of being briefed on decisions after they're made
  • Leaders who have the strategic opinion and have learned not to offer it uninvited
  • Leaders measured on velocity and output, not outcomes or strategy
  • Leaders who want real product-management capability, not just better language for the meeting
  • Anyone ready to name the office politics everyone else pretends aren't part of the job

The core claim

Four threads of influence

Organisational influence runs through four threads. The first three are the ones a rational, well-run organisation responds to. The fourth is the one a political, relationship-oriented organisation responds to whether or not it says so out loud.

01

Budget Authority

Shaping spend, not just executing against someone else's.

02

Roadmap Influence

Shaping what's next, not reacting to what's already been decided.

03

Executive Trust

Being in the room when strategy is decided, not briefed on it after.

04 · Relational

Relationship Capital

Who sponsors you, who owes you, who you owe — and whether that map is current.
