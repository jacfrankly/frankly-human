Module Overview

What We're Doing Today

Most people plan their future based on their past. This module shifts the frame.

  • Future Self Map — who you're becoming
  • Identity gap — the shift underneath
  • Behaviour gap — what your future self practices
  • Environment & support gap — what you need around you
  • Behaviour bridge — the smallest step across
02
The Frame

Your Future Self as Design Partner

Your future self is not a fantasy. It's a direction.

  • Directional — not an endpoint, a compass
  • Grounded — shaped by your real values and constraints
  • Values-aligned — already reflected in who you're becoming
  • Already emerging — showing up in small moments right now

Imagining your future self gives you direction, not pressure. It's a design partner, not a performance standard.

03
Quick Hit 01

What does your future self do that you don't yet?

Write it down. Don't edit it.

Facilitator: 60s silent writing → 2 min pair share → collect 3–4 from room. Common answers: rests more, says no earlier, creates consistently, asks for help, takes risks. Use them to land: "Your future self reveals what wants to grow in you."
Concept 01

The Future Self Map

Your future self isn't just a job or a goal. It's a whole person.

Identity
Who they are. How they show up. The story they live from.
Behaviour
What they do consistently. What they've stopped doing.
Environment
Where they work, rest, create. What surrounds them.
Relationships
Who they're close to. How they ask for help.
Work
What they do, why, and how much energy it takes.

Not a perfect version — a directional one.

05
Concept 02

Gaps Are Design Opportunities

The gap between you now and your future self is not a failure. It's a design brief.

Identity Gap
Who do you need to become? What story do you need to shift?
Behaviour Gap
What does your future self do that you don't yet?
Environment Gap
What does your future self have around them that you don't?
Support Gap
Who do they have in their corner? What support exists?

The goal is not to close all gaps. Choose the ones that matter most.

06
Activity 01
Activity 01 · Future Self Map + Gap Identification 20 min

Map your future self. Then name the gap.

  • Write your Future Self Map — 12–18 months from now. Identity, behaviour, environment, relationships, work.
  • Identify your identity gap — who do you need to become?
  • Identify your behaviour gap — what does your future self do that you don't yet?
  • Identify your environment or support gap.
  • Pairs: share the gap that feels most important.
Watch for: fantasy futures — ground gently. Work-only futures — invite whole-life thinking. Minimising gaps — normalise them. 12 min individual → 6 min pairs → 2 min group: "Which gap surprised you?"
Debrief 01

Your future self is already in motion.

Which gap feels most important to close?

Which gap feels easiest to start with?

Which gap surprised you?

Land on: "You're just catching up to the version of yourself that's already becoming."

Facilitator: Choose one debrief question based on room energy. Land on the affirmation before the break.

Concept 03

Designing the Bridge

You don't become your future self through willpower. You become them through behaviour.

Present Self
Where you are now. What you do today.
→
Behaviour Bridge
The smallest version of the behaviour that begins to close the gap.
→
Future Self
Who you're becoming. The direction you're designing toward.
  • Future self journals daily → You journal once a week.
  • Future self sets boundaries → You say no to one thing.
  • Future self creates → You create for 10 minutes.
10
Activity 02
Activity 02 · Choose a Behaviour Bridge 20 min

Choose one behaviour your future self practices. Then shrink it.

  • Choose one behaviour your future self does consistently.
  • Write the smallest version of that behaviour — the 5% version.
  • Identify what support would help you practice it this week.
  • Pairs: share your behaviour bridge.
Watch for: behaviours too big → shrink to 5% version. Self-criticism → reframe as alignment not achievement. 12 min individual → 6 min pairs → 2 min group: "What made that behaviour feel important?"
Debrief 02

Your future self becomes real the moment you act like them.

What behaviour bridges present you and future you?

What value does this behaviour honour?

What constraint does this behaviour respect?

Facilitator: Choose one question based on energy. Land on the closing line before moving to the close slide.
