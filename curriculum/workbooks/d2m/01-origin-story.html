The unconscious life brief

Most people live by a brief
they never wrote.

Common inherited briefs:

  • "Be useful."
  • "Don't be too much."
  • "Keep the peace."
  • "Achieve to be loved."
  • "Don't need anything."
03
Concept (10 min): Read each brief aloud slowly. Pause between them. Ask: "Does any of that feel familiar?" — don't take answers yet. This sets up Quick Hit 01. | Watch for: immediate recognition — people who nod or look down.

60 sec silent write · 2 min pair share · 3–4 from the room

Quick Hit 01 (5 min): 60 seconds silent. 2 minutes pairs. Collect 3–4 from the room. Common answers: achievement, harmony, safety, approval. Use them to frame: "These briefs become patterns." Move to Slide 05.
Early patterns

Patterns are adaptations.
Not flaws.

Patterns form around:

  • Roles
  • Expectations
  • Survival strategies
  • Early feedback loops
05
Concept (8 min): "Patterns are not flaws — they're adaptations." Ask: "Which early pattern still shows up today?" Take 2–3 surface-level examples only. | Watch for: people confusing a pattern with their identity — gently name the difference.
The hidden rule

Every life has a hidden rule
you never chose.

  • "Don't take up space."
  • "Don't disappoint anyone."
  • "Be the strong one."
  • "Don't ask for help."

Your hidden rule is the thing you break first
when you start designing your life.

06
Concept (7 min): "Inherited, not chosen. Naming it is the first act of authorship." Read examples slowly — same pause as the life briefs. Set up Activity 01: "Now you're going to name yours."
Activity 01 · 20 min

Life Brief → Patterns
→ Hidden Rule

Activity 01 20 min

Name the brief you inherited, the patterns you carry, and the rule you've been living by.

  1. Individual — 12 minutes silent writing
  2. Pairs — share one pattern + one hidden rule — 6 minutes
  3. Group — "What surprised you?"
Activity 01 (20 min) — Watch for: People who minimise ("It's nothing.") · People who conflate pattern with identity · People who apologise for their rule. | Reframe: "Patterns are adaptations. Hidden rules are inherited. None of this is your fault."
Debrief 01 · 10 min

Choose one.

"Which pattern feels most active today?"
"What did your hidden rule protect you from?"
"What shifts when you name it out loud?"

The 5-sentence origin story

Not a trauma dump.
Not a résumé.

Five sentences. The shape of you — what shaped you, what you learned, what you're choosing now.

Five parts:

  1. Where you came from
  2. What shaped you
  3. What you learned
  4. What you carried
  5. What you're choosing now
10
Concept (10 min): "Not a trauma dump. Not a résumé. Not a justification." Give the structure clearly. Say: "Five sentences is enough — start there." | Watch for: people who want to write paragraphs. Redirect gently before Activity 02.
Activity 02 · 20 min

Write your 5-sentence
origin story

Activity 02 20 min

Write five short sentences. The shape of where you come from and what you're choosing now.

  1. Individual — 12 minutes: write the 5 sentences
  2. Pairs — share one sentence only — the one that feels most true — 6 min
  3. Group — "What felt different about writing it this way?"
Activity 02 (20 min) — What good looks like: Short, grounded, honest. No over-explaining. No self-blame. No performance. | Watch for: Writing paragraphs → redirect to 5 sentences. Slipping into therapy → bring back to patterns, not details.
Debrief 02 · 10 min

Choose one.

"What part of your story are you ready to retire?"
"What part are you proud to carry forward?"
"What truth surprised you?"

You don't need to reject your past.
You need to update the operating system.

Debrief 02 (10 min): Choose one question. Land on: "You don't need to reject your past. You need to update the operating system." | If questions are still open: "Write them down — they're for you, not for now."
