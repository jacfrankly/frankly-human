What Design to Me is

D2M is a program about self-authorship — the ability to design a life that actually feels like yours. Not a self-help course. Not a productivity course. Not a "fix yourself" course.

It's a thinking course for your life. It applies design methodology to the hardest design challenge you'll ever work on — you. It teaches you how to see your patterns clearly, understand the systems you're living inside, and build a life that matches who you're becoming.

"Design to Me is the personal counterpart to Design-Led. Where Design-Led teaches you how to make good decisions in complex organisations, D2M teaches you how to make good decisions in the complex organisation of your life."

Who it's for

  • Mid-career professionals who are competent by every measure, quietly unsure if this is the life they meant to build
  • People at a crossroads — not broken, just ready for a different system
  • People who've done the personal development reading and still can't seem to act on it
  • People who know something needs to change but don't know what to change first
  • People who are tired of optimising and ready to redesign
The methodology

The Frankly Human Loop

Every module in D2M uses the same underlying methodology. Every exercise moves you through one or more phases of this loop. The loop isn't linear — you'll revisit phases as you go deeper.

01
See

Discover what's really going on. Surface patterns, stories, and truths you've been living around.

Origin story · Values · Identity · Energy · Patterns · Life systems
→
02
Sense

Make meaning from what you see. Identify patterns, reframe problems, clarify what matters and why.

Decision hygiene · Problem framing · Belief flips · Why statement · Impact mapping
→
03
Shape

Design possibilities. Generate options, explore future selves, define ambitions, set constraints, choose the next right move.

Future self mapping · Opportunity canvas · Ambition design · Boundary scripts · 3 future stories
→
04
Show

Prototype, test, and learn. Run small experiments, gather real data, iterate your story.

7-day prototype · Feedforward · Test & learn loops · Resilience building
→
05
Sustain

Build your Life Operating System. Rituals, rhythms, boundaries, metrics, and non-negotiables that hold.

Life OS · Personal manifesto · 90-day plan · MLF Canvas · Habit loops
The program arc

Three layers

Across 12 modules, participants move through three layers of depth. Each layer builds on the last. You can't design what you haven't first understood.

Layer 01

Awareness

Understanding the patterns, stories, values, identities, and systems that shaped you. Seeing the system you're already in.

Modules 01–05
Layer 02

Alignment

Reconnecting with what actually matters — values, energy, desire, belonging, and boundaries. Getting honest about what you want.

Modules 06, 09–11
Layer 03

Agency

Learning how to make decisions with integrity, set constraints, prototype changes, design your life intentionally, and build the Life Operating System — principles, rituals, rhythms, supports, and non-negotiables — that sustains it.

Modules 07, 08, 12

How it works

Three ways to use this

01

Present it

Each module is a scroll-snap presentation deck. Open in Present mode to facilitate live workshops. Facilitator notes are pinned at the bottom of each slide.

02

Work through it

Switch to Workbook mode and the page transforms — teaching content collapses, activities open, your responses save automatically. Print a clean A4 workbook to take home.

03

Go deeper with Claude

Each module has a Claude coaching spec — a full system prompt with exercises, deepening prompts, and distress protocols. The workbook becomes an interactive coaching conversation.
