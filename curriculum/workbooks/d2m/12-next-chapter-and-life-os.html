The next chapter is a design project

It doesn't emerge by accident.
It emerges by design.

Give it a name. A title changes how you move through it — it becomes a container, not a blur.

  • "The Year of Depth"
  • "The Season of Expansion"
  • "The Return to Self"
  • "The Quiet Rebuild"

The Chapter Map

Four moves.
One map.

Every new chapter is built from the same four moves — what you leave, what you keep, what you invite, what you build.

The four quadrants:

  1. Leaving behind
  2. Carrying forward
  3. Inviting in
  4. Building toward

Designing within constraints

Constraints aren't
the enemy. They're clarity.

Design with your constraints, not against them. Name yours — don't pretend they're not there:

  • Time
  • Energy
  • Money
  • Care responsibilities
  • Health

A plan that ignores your real constraints isn't ambitious. It's fiction.

09
Concept (8 min): "Most plans fail because they were designed for a life the person doesn't have." Ask: "What's one constraint you keep planning around instead of within?" Take 2–3 answers only. Set up the 90-Day Plan.
The 90-day plan

Small enough to be real.
Big enough to matter.

  • Three focus areas — not ten
  • Clear goals — not vague hopes
  • Actionable steps — not intentions
  • Sustainable pacing — not a sprint
10
Concept (8 min): "Ninety days is long enough to matter, short enough to stay honest." Emphasise: three focus areas maximum. Watch for people wanting to plan the whole year — redirect to 90 days.

Integration

Now it's time to integrate. Your Life Operating System is made of:

  • Principles
  • Rituals
  • Rhythms
  • Constraints
  • Supports
  • Non-negotiables
14
Concept (8 min): "A plan tells you what to do. An operating system tells you how to keep being you while you do it." Name all six components, but flag: "We're building three of these today — principles, rituals, non-negotiables. The rest extend from there."
Principles, rituals, non-negotiables

Your compass.
Your anchors. Your edges.

Principles

Rules you choose to live by.

  • "I honour my energy."
  • "I don't abandon myself."
  • "I tell the truth kindly."
  • "I design within my constraints."

→ Your internal compass.

Rituals

Anchors that stabilise your system.

  • Weekly planning
  • Daily check-in
  • Monthly reflection
  • Phone-free mornings
  • Sunday reset

→ Consistency.

Non-negotiables

Boundaries that protect your life.

  • Sleep
  • Movement
  • Creative time
  • No work after 6pm
  • One night a week alone

→ Integrity.

15
Concept (10 min): Walk through each column with its outcome line — "Principles give you a compass. Rituals give you consistency. Non-negotiables give you integrity." Read a few examples aloud. Set up Activity 03.

Your personal manifesto

A declaration.
Not a summary.

This is your anchor — the thing you return to on the hard days, the version of you that isn't performing for anyone.

  • What you stand for
  • What you refuse to shrink for
  • What you're building toward
  • What you're done carrying
  • What your life gets to feel like now

Five to seven lines. Not a polished paragraph — a set of true, declarative sentences.
