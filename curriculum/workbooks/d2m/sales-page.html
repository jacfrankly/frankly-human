The pattern

You know the shape of it already

  • Responsibilities pile on. The to-do list never actually ends.
  • You're good at your life, by every external measure — and still flat about it.
  • The goalposts keep moving, because that's what being human does. You live, you grow, you change, you want different things. You are never going to be "done."
  • Somewhere underneath it, a voice keeps saying: more than this.

Most advice tells you to add something — a habit, a hobby, a five-year plan. This isn't that. It's not about doing more. It's about learning what's actually yours to want, and building the life around it — on purpose, not by accident.

How we get there — the Why Workbook

"This is a draft, not a destination. A compass, not a contract."

Before the program starts, you go through the Why Workbook — a short, guided reflection, not a form. It takes the patterns you're already living and names the specific thing holding you back, in your own words. You leave with a Why Statement — a first draft that becomes the anchor for everything that follows in Design to Me.

Welcome to Design to Me

A thinking course for your life

A life-design curriculum for people who are ready to live with intention, integrity, and agency. Not a self-help course. Not a productivity course. Not a "fix yourself" course.

You'll learn how to see your patterns clearly, understand the systems you're living inside, make decisions with integrity, honour your energy and your constraints, build a life that matches who you're becoming, create rituals and rhythms that actually sustain you, and design your next chapter with intention.

Your life is a system, and systems produce predictable outcomes. If you want different outcomes, you don't need more willpower — you need a different system.

This program gives you the tools to redesign it.

Q + A

Before you decide

Who's this for?

This is for you if you're ready to do the work, not just consume the content — to own your story instead of performing it, build a more honest relationship with your own time and energy, and design a life you'd actually choose, not just the one that happened to you. And if you want to feel more confident, less compromised in your daily choices, able to ask for what you actually need, and more resilient going after the things that interest you.

What if I actually like my life and don't want to blow it up?

Good — this isn't a demolition. It's not about adding more for the sake of more; it's about less, but better. The goal is to design a life that's genuinely yours, which includes keeping and strengthening the parts that already work.

What if I have no idea what I want?

That's the normal starting point, not the exception. The Why Workbook and Module 01 exist precisely because most people can't answer "what do I want" cold — they can only tell you what's been nagging at them. That's enough to start.

I don't have time for this.

Fair, and we won't pretend you can manufacture more of it. What the program actually asks for is a weekly hour and honest attention, not a life overhaul. Part of Module 4 is specifically about telling the truth about your real capacity instead of the capacity you wish you had.

I'm close to burnout. Should I wait?

If you're in the acute phase of burnout, rest first — this program assumes enough baseline capacity to reflect and experiment, and it won't work as a substitute for recovery. Once you're back to something closer to neutral, this is exactly the next step.

What if I don't get results?

Here's the honest trade-off: this program can't guarantee an outcome, because the whole point is that you're building your own capacity to decide, not following a script. What it does guarantee is process — you'll be testing ideas, gathering real feedback, and making decisions with more clarity than you started with. That's the mechanism, not a promise about where you land.
