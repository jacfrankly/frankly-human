Module 04 · Overview

What this module
is about

  • Energy leaks — where your energy drains fastest
  • Energy sources — what reliably restores you
  • Capacity constraints — the real limits that shape your life
  • Capacity shifts — designing within reality, not against it
Frame: "Most people plan their life as if they have infinite capacity. But your life is designed by what you actually have energy for." | Set up the multi-domain energy model before moving on.
Concept · Energy

Energy is
multi-dimensional

Physical
Sleep, movement, food, illness, pain, chronic load
Emotional
Relationships, care, grief, conflict, emotional labour
Mental
Decisions, complexity, context-switching, cognitive load
Creative
Making, thinking, expression, play, curiosity, meaning

Most people track one domain and wonder why they're depleted in the others.

Ask: "Which domain feels most depleted for you lately?" Don't take answers yet — sets up Quick Hit 01. | Watch for: people only naming physical energy — invite emotional, mental, and creative.

Quick Hit · 5 min
"

Where does your energy leak most consistently?

Write it down. Don't edit it.

Timer: 60 sec silent writing · 2 min pair share · collect 3–4 from the room. | Common answers: overfunctioning, people-pleasing, context-switching, emotional labour. | Land on: "Energy leaks aren't failures — they're signals."
Concept · Energy Leaks

Leaks are signals,
not failures

Leaks show up as:

  • Overfunctioning — doing what others could do
  • People-pleasing — overriding your own needs
  • Perfectionism — spending 80% for 5% return
  • Avoidance — the cost of not deciding
  • Emotional labour — carrying what isn't yours

Leaks are often inherited or rewarded. They cost more than time — they cost identity and integrity.

Ask: "What does your biggest leak cost you?" Take 2–3 examples. | Reframe any self-blame: "Energy leaks are information — what is this one telling you?"
Concept · Energy Sources

What restores you

Sources reliably restore energy:

  • Rest & sleep
  • Solitude
  • Connection
  • Creativity & play
  • Movement
  • Nature
  • Structure & rhythm
  • Meaning & contribution

Most people underestimate how little they need to feel restored.

Set up Activity 01. | Watch for: people listing "should" sources — redirect to what actually restores them. | "Energy sources aren't indulgent — they're fuel."
Activity 01 · 20 min
Activity 01 — Individual + Pairs + Group 20 min

Map your Energy: Leaks → Sources → Patterns

  • List your energy leaks — where you consistently lose energy
  • List your energy sources — what reliably restores you
  • Notice patterns across the four domains
  • Pairs (6 min): share one leak + one source
  • Group (2 min): "What surprised you about your energy map?"
Watch for: Moralising leaks → "Energy leaks are information." | "Should" sources → redirect to what actually restores. | Only naming physical energy → invite emotional, mental, creative domains.

Concept · Capacity

Constraints are
design parameters

Real constraints include:

  • Time
  • Money
  • Health
  • Care responsibilities
  • Bandwidth
  • Emotional load

Constraints aren't weaknesses.
They're design parameters.

Designing against constraints creates burnout.
Designing with them creates sustainability.

Ask: "What constraint do you need to design with, not against?" | Sets up Activity 02. | Normalise: "Constraints aren't excuses — they're the reality your design has to fit."
Activity 02 · 20 min
Activity 02 — Individual + Pairs + Group 20 min

Name your Capacity Constraint + Choose a Capacity Shift

  • Name one real capacity constraint you've been designing against
  • Write what happens when you ignore it
  • Choose one shift that honours your real bandwidth
  • Pairs (6 min): share the shift
  • Group (2 min): "What made that shift feel important?"
Watch for: Shifts too big → "What's the 5% version?" | People minimising constraints → normalise them. | Self-criticism → "This isn't about discipline — it's about design."

What value does this shift protect?

Land on: "You don't need more discipline. You need a life that fits your energy." | This lands hard for most rooms — let it breathe.
