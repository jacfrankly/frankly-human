# Frankly Human — Promotions Reference

Working doc of audience-framing ideas and finalized copy, for the library series and future community-facing promotion.

---

## Finalized bio line (community-facing)

> Frankly Human is a life and business coaching practice run by Jacinta McMahon, a local design leader with 20 years' experience helping people work out what's actually next, whether that's a career shift, a business idea, or just wanting to feel more like themselves again.

**Principle behind it:** describe the feeling, not the category. Naming a tidy list of situations (career change, new business, job decisions) risks someone whose actual feeling doesn't fit neatly reading it and self-selecting out, even though the workshop would help them the same way. Three concrete, recognisable examples, then an open door ("or just wanting to feel more like themselves again") for everyone else.

## Audience-framing ideas (for future flyers, posts, and variations)

Don't cram all of these into one piece of promotion, pick two or three that feel most true to the actual audience for that specific placement, rather than trying to cover everyone at once.

- A career shift, deciding what's next professionally
- A business idea, ready to build something of their own
- Just wanting to feel more like themselves again
- Returning to work after time away
- Feeling flat despite things "going fine" on paper
- Considering study or upskilling
- Quietly resenting a role they used to love
- The empty-nest or midlife recalibration moment

## Open question: certifications

**Certified Life Coach (Wild Success), Human Design Practitioner (The Inner Institute), Mindfulness Practitioner (Melbourne Meditation Centre)**

Worth applying these selectively by audience, not adding all three uniformly across every piece of Frankly Human material. Two different things pull in different directions here:

**For the library series and Come Home to Yourself generally**, all three fit naturally. This is a reflective, values-based, community context, mindfulness and life coaching credentials add real, relevant credibility here, and the audience isn't being asked to trust Frankly Human with a commercial decision.

**For Regional Business Leader and both Design-Led tracks, worth real caution, particularly on Human Design specifically.** The positioning built across this whole body of work, "not a soft issue, it's a strategic one," "warm clarity with a backbone," commercially fluent language for business and design-leadership audiences, has been deliberately built to read as credible and evidence-grounded to a skeptical enterprise or business-owner reader. Human Design (birth-chart-based personality typing) sits in a different register entirely, and listing it alongside the design-leadership and Macquarie career credibility risks undercutting exactly the "this isn't soft" distinction that's been carefully established for those audiences. Worth testing this concern against real reactions before deciding, this is a judgment call, not a settled fact, but it's a real risk worth naming rather than defaulting into.

**Suggested approach:** track-specific bios, not one universal one.
- Community/Come Home to Yourself bio: can include all three certifications
- Regional Business Leader / Design-Led bios: lead with the design-leadership and enterprise career credibility; consider whether Human Design belongs here at all, or whether Certified Life Coach and Mindfulness Practitioner alone strike the right balance without it

Not a final decision, worth sitting with before locking in.
