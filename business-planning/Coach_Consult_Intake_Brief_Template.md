# Frankly Human — First Coach/Consult Intake Brief

Standard template for capturing the brief before any first paid coaching or consulting conversation (Arm 2: Partnership Engagements). Fill this out before the call using whatever's already known, then complete it live during the conversation. Reusable for every new client, not specific to any one engagement type.

## Opening: Strengths & Aspirations (SOAR-based, ask before Situation)

Start here, not with the problem. Softens the entry the same way "...is that me?" does for the diagnostics, just from the positive side rather than the normalizing side.

- What are you most proud of, in the work you've done?
- What do others compliment you on?
- What's important to you right now?
- What roles or work genuinely attract you?

# Example: Miranda Burger — First Call Prep

Filled in with what's already known ahead of the call, as the first real use of this template.

## Client Snapshot
- **Name:** Miranda Burger
- **Role / organisation:** Head of Brand & Design at Raleru (trading as Camera House)
- **How they found Frankly Human:** LinkedIn Services request
- **Referral source or context:** Submitted a service request for leadership coaching, strategy-focused, three days before first contact

## Situation
- Current role: leads brand and design for a 50+ location retail franchise, spearheaded a full rebrand
- What prompted the request: unclear yet, worth asking directly, could be role-related, could be about what's next
- Her own words (from the request): "Brand and retail are my passion"; goal type: "Strategy"; audience: "Self," not employees

## Goal
- **Stated goal (her words):** leadership coaching, strategy-focused
- **Underlying goal (to confirm live):** her own About bio suggests she wants a role or position that "recognises strategic impact alongside craft," not just creative execution, worth testing whether the coaching goal is about her current role or about positioning for what's next

## Blockers
- Unknown yet, this is the heart of the first call, don't guess ahead of time, ask directly and listen for which category she reaches for first

## Pattern Notes
- Her About reads close to the capability-and-credibility gap Design-Led: Leader is built around, built real structural change (board-level buy-in across a distributed network) but is naming a gap in how that's recognised, worth listening for whether this matches Service Department Trap or Silent Expert without suggesting either first

## Recommended Next Step
- To be determined live, don't decide before the conversation
