# Library Series — Pitch and Flyer Copy

Two separate pieces: the pitch to send the library coordinator, and the final wording for the flyer itself.

---

## 1. THE PITCH (email to the library coordinator)

Subject: Proposal — free community workshop series at [Library Name]

Hi [Coordinator name],

I'd like to propose running a free, three-week community workshop series at [Library Name], and I understand you're able to offer the space at no cost for genuine community programming.

A little about me and the series:

Frankly Human is a life and business coaching practice I run alongside a senior design leadership career spanning twenty years. This series would be offered entirely free, as a genuine contribution to the local community, not a promotion for any paid program.

The series is for local women working out what's actually next, whether that's a career shift, a business idea, or just wanting to feel more like themselves again. Drawing on values-based coaching methodology, participants would work through a structured, three-week reflective process and leave with a written "Why Statement," a clear sense of what actually matters to them.

Proposed structure:
- Week 1: What's really been on your mind
- Week 2: What you value, and where it's missing
- Week 3: Writing your own "why," and one small step forward

I'd expect each session to run around 60–90 minutes, ideally the same time and day each week so participants can commit to the full series. I'm flexible on exact scheduling and happy to work around what suits the library.

Could we find a time to talk through logistics, room availability, capacity, and how you'd like this listed in your community programming?

Thanks for considering it,
Jacinta McMahon
Frankly Human
